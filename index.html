UNCOMPROMISING SELECTION

START WITH THE GOOD STUFF.

No improvers. No artificial enzymes. Just six honest ingredients treated with reverence.

STONEGROUND FLOUR

Milled slowly on granite stones to preserve the wheat germ, natural oils and earthy fragrance.

CULTURED BUTTER

84% butterfat content, churned from grass-fed cream and fermented with lactic cultures for unmatched depth.

FARM EGGS

Gathered daily from local pasture-raised hens with deep amber yolks that lend brilliant gold to our brioche.

WILD HONEY

Raw unfiltered mountain nectar infused with wildflower notes, providing subtle natural sweetness.

DARK COCOA

70% single-origin Belgian chocolate batons, rich in fruit notes and bitter-sweet harmony.

SEASONAL BERRIES

Hand-picked organic wild raspberries, blackberries and blueberries delivered fresh each morning.
